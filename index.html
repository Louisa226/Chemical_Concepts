Stage gates

Five gates between an idea and a certificate of analysis.

Most formulation work dies in the handoffs — bench notebook to simulation to pilot to plant, each with its own file and its own version of the truth. Reflux keeps one record and moves it through the gates with you.

GATE 01

Formulate

Build a recipe from a governed raw-material library. Every component carries its own property set, cost curve, and supplier lot history.

1 g – 2 kg
GATE 02

Predict

Thermophysical properties resolve as you type — viscosity by Kendall–Monroe, density by ideal-volume mixing, flash point by dilution model.

In silico
GATE 03

Screen

Regulatory and hazard checks run against the live composition. Restricted substances flag at the component level, before you order drums.

42 jurisdictions
GATE 04

Scale

Translate bench ratios into charge sheets: mixer geometry, heat duty, addition order, and hold times sized for the vessel you actually own.

200 L – 40 m³

Release

Batch records, deviations, and CoAs generate from the same recipe object. Lot genealogy traces backward to the drum it came from.

Platform

Built by process engineers, for the parts of the job nobody automates.

No generic PLM shoehorned into a lab. Every module below exists because a formulation team asked for it after losing a week to a spreadsheet.

Property prediction

Viscosity, density, flash point, pH, VOC, and cost resolve from composition using published mixing rules — not curve-fits on your last twelve batches.

Kendall–Monroe · UNIFAC · NRTL

Application fit

Score a composition against end-use markets before you commit to one. Useful when a customer trial fails and you need to know where else the chemistry sells.

9 markets · live scoring

Regulatory screening

Components check against REACH Annex XVII, TSCA inventory, Prop 65, and regional VOC caps. Safety data sheets draft in 28 languages and stay tied to the recipe version.

GHS rev. 10 · SDS authoring

Scale-up modelling

Hold constant tip speed or constant power per volume and Reflux resizes the charge sheet for your vessel — with jacket duty, ramp rates, and addition order carried along.

Bench → pilot → plant

Plant connectors

Pull real batch data back into the recipe. OPC UA, Modbus TCP, and historian connectors close the loop so predicted properties get graded against what the plant actually made.

OPC UA · Modbus · PI / AVEVA

Cost & supply

Live landed cost per kilogram against contracted supplier pricing, with substitution candidates ranked by property delta when a raw material goes on allocation.
